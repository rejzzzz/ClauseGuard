# Vendor Master Services Agreement (MSA) Playbook

## 1. Limitation of Liability
- **Standard Position:** Mutual limitation of liability capped at 1x the total fees paid under the agreement in the preceding 12 months.
- **Fallback Position:** Mutual cap up to 2x fees paid. Never accept uncapped liability except for indemnification, confidentiality breaches, or gross negligence.
- **Unacceptable:** Unilateral vendor cap while customer liability remains uncapped.

## 2. Indemnification
- **Standard Position:** Vendor indemnifies Customer against third-party intellectual property infringement claims and data breach incidents.
- **Fallback Position:** Cap IP indemnification at $1,000,000.
- **Unacceptable:** Customer indemnifying Vendor for general use of Vendor's software or services.

## 3. Governing Law & Jurisdiction
- **Standard Position:** Delaware law and jurisdiction.
- **Fallback Position:** New York or California law and jurisdiction.
- **Unacceptable:** Non-US jurisdictions or binding arbitration without opt-out.
